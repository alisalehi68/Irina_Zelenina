About the artist

Between the studio and the street, the same question — what does the eye keep?

Irina Zelenina is an oil painter based in Munich, working between the studio and plein air. Her canvases sit at the edge of photograph and memory — figures rendered with quiet, deliberate brushwork, then softened by the same fading that time does to a face.

Her paintings have been shown across galleries and open studios, and are held in private collections in Germany and abroad. Each new work begins the same way: a moment worth not forgetting.
